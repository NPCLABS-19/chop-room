Perform

Hold Shift for rhythmic repeat, Q for low pass, W for echo, E for drive. Effects work while pads or notes are held. Gate stops a chop when you release it.

Sample YouTube

Load a link and open the video. In Chrome or Edge, choose Capture tab audio, select the YouTube tab, and enable Share tab audio. Play the video in the shared tab. The timer starts when sound arrives and automatically finishes the selected duration. Finish & chop ends it sooner. Leading and trailing silence are removed before the recording is chopped into 16 pads. Audio file loading is also available.

Loop

Record starts at beat one. A second press ends recording and plays the loop. Press Record during playback to overdub. BPM and length are locked during playback. Export mixes all unmuted layers into a WAV.
